# Privacy policy

Geo Trail Maps — effective 9 November 2026

Geo Trail Maps has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting. The developer does not receive any data from the app.

## Location

The app uses your device's location to show where you are on a map and, when you record, to build a track. Recording continues in the background until you stop it, with a notification shown if notifications are allowed for the app. Location is processed on your device and is never sent to the developer. Recorded tracks are stored like your other data (see below), so Android backup and files you export can include them.

## Data stored on your device

Imported maps, recorded tracks, features you add, and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They stay there until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

If Android backup or device-to-device transfer is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this data in your Google backup or copy it to a new phone. Large maps can exceed Android's backup size limit and be left out.

## Background map tiles

When a background map is shown and the phone is online, the app downloads map tiles directly from the selected provider:

- OpenStreetMap: `tile.openstreetmap.org`, operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation ([privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy))
- Satellite: `tiles.maps.eox.at`, operated by EOX IT Services GmbH
- A map source you added in Settings: the server in the tile address you entered

Each request reveals your IP address and the area of the map you are viewing. When the map follows your position, that area is near where you are. The provider's own privacy policy applies to these requests. Downloaded tiles are cached on your device.

Each map has its own background map. A map you open for the first time uses the default set under "Background map for new maps" in Settings, which is OpenStreetMap on a new install. If you updated from an earlier version, the default is the background you chose last. To stop tile requests, set that default to None, and set the background map to None under Layers on any map that already shows one.

## Exporting and sharing

When you export or share features, tracks or maps, the app hands the file to the app or location you choose. What happens to it after that is up to you and that app.
